# ShopEase Returns and Refunds FAQs

## Can I return a product?
Eligible products can be returned within 30 days of delivery.

## What products cannot be returned?
Products marked as non-returnable, personalized products, and certain hygiene-related products may not be eligible for return.

## How do I request a return?
Open the Orders section, select the relevant order, and choose "Request Return." You will be asked to select which items you're returning and pick a reason from a short list, such as "wrong size," "arrived damaged," or "no longer needed," since the reason you choose affects whether return shipping is free. Once submitted, most requests are auto-approved within minutes for standard items, after which a prepaid return label is emailed to you; items flagged as damaged or incorrect may take slightly longer as they can be routed to a support agent for a quick manual review first.

## Who pays for return shipping?
Return shipping policies depend on the reason for the return. Products that arrive damaged or incorrect may qualify for free return shipping.

## How long does a refund take?
After the returned product is inspected and approved, the refund is normally processed within 5 to 7 business days. This timeframe begins once the item physically arrives at our returns warehouse and passes quality inspection, not from the day you shipped it, so transit time to the warehouse should be added on top of the 5 to 7 business days. Once the refund is processed on our end, most banks and card issuers take an additional 2 to 5 business days to actually post the funds back to your statement, so the total time from drop-off to seeing the money can occasionally stretch to two full weeks depending on your bank.

## Where will my refund be sent?
Refunds are generally returned to the original payment method, meaning a credit card purchase is refunded to that same card, a PayPal purchase back to PayPal, and so on, since this is the standard practice used to prevent fraud. If the original payment method has since expired, been cancelled, or is otherwise unavailable (such as an expired credit card), the refund will instead be converted into ShopEase Store Credit automatically, which never expires and can be applied to any future purchase at checkout. Customers can also proactively choose Store Credit instead of an original-method refund during the return request process if they prefer a faster turnaround, since store credit is typically issued immediately upon inspection rather than waiting on bank processing times.

## What if I receive a damaged product?
Contact customer support within 7 days of delivery and provide photos of the damaged product and the package, including close-up shots of the specific damage and a wider shot showing the box and any packaging materials, since this helps determine whether the damage happened during manufacturing, warehouse handling, or transit. Damaged items are always eligible for free return shipping regardless of the item's normal return policy, and in most cases we will dispatch a replacement immediately rather than waiting for the damaged item to be returned first. If the exact same item is out of stock, you will be offered either a full refund or store credit at your choice.

## Can I exchange a product?
Exchanges are available for selected products subject to stock availability, and are most commonly used for swapping a different size or color of the same item rather than exchanging for a completely different product. To start one, choose "Exchange" instead of "Return for Refund" when submitting your return request; if the replacement variant is in stock at the same price, no additional payment is required and the new item ships out as soon as the original is confirmed for return. If the desired replacement is no longer available or has a different price, the system will instead guide you toward a standard refund or store credit so you can reorder the item you actually want.

## Do returned products need to be in their original packaging?
Yes. Returned items must be unused, unwashed, and packed in original boxes with all original tags, manuals, and accessories attached.

## Can I return an item purchased with a promotional code or gift card?
Yes. Items purchased with discount codes or gift cards are returnable; refunds will reflect the actual discounted amount paid.

## What is your return policy for clearance or final sale items?
Items explicitly labeled as "Final Sale" or "Clearance" cannot be returned, refunded, or exchanged unless defective on arrival.

## How do I print my prepaid return shipping label?
Once your return request is approved in the Orders portal, a downloadable PDF label is generated and emailed to you for printing.

## Can I return items from multiple orders in one box?
No. Items from separate orders must be packed in separate boxes with their respective return authorization slips to avoid processing delays.

## Where can I drop off my return shipment?
Drop off your labeled package at any authorized carrier depot, post office, or parcel drop box specified on the return label.

## Can I request a courier pickup for my return from my home?
Yes. In supported areas, home return pickups can be scheduled directly through the carrier's online scheduling tool for a small carrier fee.

## Does ShopEase charge a restocking fee for returns?
ShopEase does not charge restocking fees for general consumer goods, though heavy freight items or large appliances may incur a 15% fee.

## What happens if my return package is lost by the carrier in transit?
Retain your courier drop-off receipt. If the package is lost in transit, support will honor the refund once drop-off confirmation is verified.

## How do I track the progress of my return and inspection?
You can follow the return journey in your account under Orders > Return Details, from warehouse delivery through final quality inspection.

## Can I choose store credit or a ShopEase gift card instead of an original payment refund?
Yes. When submitting a return request, you may elect to receive ShopEase Store Credit, which is issued immediately upon inspection.

## Are original shipping charges refunded when I return an item?
Original shipping fees are non-refundable unless the return is due to a ShopEase fulfillment error or a defective product.

## What happens if a returned item fails the quality inspection?
Items returned damaged through misuse, missing parts, or showing signs of heavy wear may be sent back to you or subject to a partial deduction.

## Can I exchange an item for a different size, style, or color?
Yes. If the replacement size or color is in stock at the identical price, select "Exchange" rather than "Return for Refund" during the request.

## How do I return an item that was received as a gift?
Recipients can initiate a gift return using the order number and delivery zip code. Refunds for gift returns are issued as ShopEase Store Credit.

## How do refunds work for items purchased as part of a bundle or BOGO sale?
If returning part of a bundle or "Buy One Get One" deal, the refund is adjusted proportionally to reflect the regular price of retained items.

## What is the return window for electronics and high-value tech devices?
Electronics, computers, and mobile devices have a 14-day return window from the date of delivery and must be factory reset before shipping back.

## Does ShopEase provide a warranty on defective products past the standard return window?
Defective items outside the 30-day window are covered under individual manufacturer warranties; support can assist in providing purchase documentation.

## What should I do if my refund amount appears incorrect?
Review your return confirmation for deducted shipping fees or promotional adjustments. If discrepancies remain, contact customer support.

## Can I cancel a return request after initiating it?
Yes. If you decide to keep the item, simply disregard the return label; the return request automatically expires after 30 days of inactivity.

## What happens if I accidentally return an item not purchased from ShopEase?
Contact customer support immediately. While we attempt to locate accidental parcels, ShopEase is not responsible for non-inventory items received.

## How many days do I have to mail my return after receiving the return label?
Return labels must be scanned by the designated carrier within 14 days of label generation to remain valid.

## Does ShopEase offer price matching or post-purchase price adjustments?
If an item you purchased drops in price on ShopEase within 7 days of delivery, contact support to receive a refund for the price difference.
