# ShopEase Orders FAQs

## How can I place an order?
Add the products you want to your shopping cart, proceed to checkout, enter your shipping information, select a payment method, and confirm your order.

## Can I cancel my order?
An order can be cancelled before it enters the shipping process. Once the order has been shipped, cancellation is no longer available, since the package is already in the carrier's hands and cannot be recalled from our side. To cancel, open the Orders section, select the relevant order, and look for a "Cancel Order" button, which only appears while the order status still reads "Processing" or "On Hold." If the button is no longer visible, it usually means the order has already moved into the fulfillment or shipping stage, in which case your best option is to wait for delivery and then start a return instead.

## How can I check my order status?
Open the Orders section in your account and select the order you want to track. Each order will display one of several statuses: "Processing" (payment cleared, items being prepared), "On Hold" (awaiting payment or address verification), "Shipped" (in transit with a carrier), or "Delivered" (confirmed by the carrier as received). Once a status changes to "Shipped," a tracking number and a direct link to the carrier's tracking page will also appear on the same order details screen, allowing you to follow its journey in real time without needing to contact support.

## Can I change my shipping address after ordering?
The shipping address can only be changed before the order enters the shipping process, which usually means within a short window right after checkout, before fulfillment begins picking your items. To request the change, go to the Orders section, open the order in question, and select "Edit Shipping Address" if it is still available; once fulfillment has started, this option will disappear from the order details page. If your order has already shipped and the address was incorrect, you will need to contact customer support immediately, since redirecting an in-transit package is not always possible depending on the carrier and may result in the package being returned to our warehouse instead.

## What should I do if I received the wrong product?
Contact customer support within 7 days of delivery and provide the order number and a description of the incorrect item.

## What should I do if my order is missing?
Check the tracking information first, since many "missing" packages are actually still in transit or were delivered to a nearby location such as a porch, mailroom, or neighbor's doorstep. If the order is marked as delivered but you have not received it, wait up to 24 hours, as carriers sometimes mark packages as delivered slightly before the driver's final drop-off, and then contact customer support with your order number if it still hasn't turned up. Support can open an official carrier trace investigation and, in most cases, will issue a replacement or refund once the investigation confirms the package was genuinely lost.

## Can I modify item quantities or colors after placing an order?
Orders cannot be edited once placed. If the order has not yet entered fulfillment, you may cancel it and place a new order with the correct items.

## Why was my order cancelled automatically?
Orders may be cancelled automatically due to unexpected inventory shortages, payment verification failures, or suspected fraud triggers.

## What payment methods does ShopEase accept?
ShopEase accepts major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover), PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and ShopEase Gift Cards.

## When will my payment method be charged?
Your payment method is authorized at checkout and fully charged once your items are prepared for dispatch.

## How do I apply a discount code or coupon to my order?
Enter your promotional code into the "Promo Code" field in the order summary section during checkout and click "Apply."

## Can I combine multiple discount codes on one order?
Only one promotional coupon code can be applied per order unless a specific promotion explicitly states otherwise.

## Why is my promo code not applying at checkout?
Promo codes may fail if they have expired, do not meet minimum order thresholds, or exclude certain sale or brand items.

## Where can I find and download my official order invoice?
Navigate to the Orders section, click the relevant order number, and select "Download PDF Invoice."

## Can I combine two separate orders into a single shipment?
Orders placed separately cannot be consolidated after checkout because fulfillment begins immediately for each order ID.

## Can I ship items in a single order to multiple addresses?
No. Each order can only be shipped to a single destination address. To ship to multiple addresses, please submit separate orders.

## What does the "Processing" order status mean?
"Processing" indicates that payment has cleared and our fulfillment center is picking, packing, and preparing your items for courier dispatch.

## What does the "On Hold" order status mean?
An "On Hold" status usually signifies that payment authentication is pending or additional address verification is needed before release.

## How do back-orders and pre-orders work?
Pre-ordered or back-ordered items are charged at checkout and will ship as soon as stock arrives at our warehouse. Estimated dates appear on the product page.

## Are items reserved in my cart while I browse?
No. Items placed in your shopping cart are not reserved and remain available for other shoppers until checkout is successfully completed.

## Is there a minimum or maximum purchase limit per order?
There is no minimum purchase requirement, but certain high-demand or promotional products may carry per-customer quantity limits.

## Can I place an order by phone or through live chat?
For customer security, payment information cannot be collected by phone or chat; all orders must be placed securely through the website or mobile app.

## What should I do if my payment was declined at checkout?
Verify your billing details, expiration date, and CVV code, or contact your issuing bank. You may also select an alternative payment method.

## Can I purchase an order as a gift for someone else?
Yes. Enter the recipient's shipping address at checkout and tick the "This order is a gift" box to conceal price details on the packing slip.

## Does ShopEase offer gift wrapping or custom gift messages?
Yes. Eligible items include an option to add gift wrapping and an optional 200-character gift message during the checkout process.

## Does ShopEase collect sales tax or VAT on orders?
Applicable state, local, or value-added taxes are calculated automatically during checkout based on the destination delivery address.

## What should I do if my card shows duplicate pending charges?
Duplicate entries are usually temporary authorization holds that fall off within 2 to 5 business days. If charges post permanently, contact support.

## How can I quickly re-order items I bought previously?
Go to your Orders history, find the original purchase, and click the "Buy Again" button to add identical items straight to your cart.

## What happens if an item becomes unavailable after I place my order?
If an item goes out of stock during fulfillment, you will be notified promptly, the item will be cancelled from the order, and a full refund will be issued.

## How do I confirm that my order went through successfully?
After placing an order, you will see a confirmation screen with an order number and receive an automated order confirmation email within minutes.

## Can I purchase and redeem ShopEase electronic gift cards?
Yes. Digital gift cards are available in various denominations, delivered via email, and redeemable using the unique code during checkout.
