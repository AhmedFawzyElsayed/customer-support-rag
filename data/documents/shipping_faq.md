# ShopEase Shipping FAQs

## How long does standard shipping take?
Standard shipping usually takes 3 to 5 business days after the order has been processed, not counting the 1 to 2 business days needed for order processing itself. This means the total time from placing your order to it arriving at your door is typically 4 to 7 business days in total. Delivery estimates are calculated based on business days only, so orders placed on a Friday evening will generally begin their transit countdown on the following Monday. Customers in more remote or rural zip codes may occasionally see an extra 1 to 2 days added to the standard window due to last-mile carrier routing.

## How long does express shipping take?
Express shipping usually takes 1 to 2 business days after processing, making it the fastest guaranteed option apart from same-day or overnight delivery in eligible metro areas. Express orders are prioritized in our fulfillment centers, meaning they typically skip the standard queue and are picked and packed within a few hours of payment confirmation. Because of this priority handling, express shipping carries an additional fee at checkout, which varies depending on package weight and destination distance, and the fee is refundable if the guaranteed delivery window is missed due to a carrier delay.

## Do you provide tracking?
Yes. A tracking number is provided after the package is shipped.

## Why is my package delayed?
Shipping delays can occur because of weather, carrier issues, high-volume periods, or incorrect delivery information.

## Can I track my order?
Yes. Customers can track their order from the Orders section using the tracking number provided after shipment.

## Do you ship internationally?
International shipping availability depends on the destination country and product eligibility, since some products are restricted from export due to local regulations, battery-content rules, or licensing agreements with manufacturers. When you enter a shipping address at checkout, the system automatically checks which items in your cart can legally and logistically be shipped to that country, and will flag or remove any item that isn't eligible before you complete your purchase. International orders generally take longer than domestic ones, often 7 to 21 business days depending on the destination and customs processing, and may be split into multiple shipments if items ship from different regional warehouses.

## What happens if nobody is available to receive the package?
The delivery carrier may attempt another delivery or provide instructions for collection depending on the destination.

## How much does standard shipping cost?
Standard shipping costs a flat rate of $4.99 on orders below the free shipping threshold.

## Does ShopEase offer free shipping?
Yes. ShopEase provides free standard shipping on domestic orders with a subtotal of $50 or more before taxes.

## Which courier and shipping carriers do you use?
ShopEase partners with major carriers including UPS, FedEx, DHL, and local postal services depending on package size and destination.

## Do you deliver to P.O. Boxes and APO/FPO addresses?
Yes. Deliveries to P.O. Boxes and military APO/FPO locations are handled via national postal mail services.

## Do you provide same-day or overnight delivery?
Same-day delivery is available in select metropolitan zip codes for orders placed before 11:00 AM local time. Overnight delivery is available nationwide.

## What is the difference between order processing time and shipping transit time?
Processing time is the 1 to 2 business days required to prepare your order, while transit time is the duration the courier takes to deliver it.

## Why does my tracking number show "Label Created" or no movement?
"Label Created" means the shipment label was generated and the package is waiting for courier pickup or first transit scan. Please allow up to 24 hours.

## Can I update my delivery instructions while my package is in transit?
Yes. Use the carrier tracking portal via the link in your shipping confirmation to provide gate codes, drop-off locations, or delivery notes.

## Can I hold a shipment at a local carrier depot or locker?
Yes. Once your tracking number is active, visit the carrier's tracking page to request a hold for pickup at an authorized access point or locker.

## Why was my order divided into multiple packages?
Items may ship from different regional fulfillment centers or in separate boxes if they differ significantly in size or warehouse availability.

## Will I be charged extra fees if an order arrives in separate shipments?
No. Additional split shipments for the same order do not incur extra shipping charges beyond the initial checkout rate.

## Who pays for customs duties, taxes, and import tariffs on international orders?
International orders may incur local customs duties, taxes, and handling fees, which are the responsibility of the recipient upon arrival unless prepaid.

## Is a signature required upon delivery?
Signatures are generally not required for standard deliveries, but high-value orders exceeding $300 may require an adult signature.

## What should I do if tracking says "Delivered" but I cannot find my package?
Check porches, side entrances, building mailrooms, and with neighbors. If not located after 24 hours, contact customer support.

## What happens if a shipment is returned to sender due to an incorrect address?
Once the carrier returns an undeliverable package to our warehouse, a refund is issued for the items minus initial carrier handling costs.

## Can I upgrade my shipping speed after placing an order?
Shipping speeds cannot be modified once checkout is finished and order processing has begun.

## Does ShopEase deliver on weekends and federal holidays?
Standard delivery typically runs Monday through Friday, though select couriers offer Saturday residential drop-offs. Deliveries are paused on holidays.

## Are there restrictions on shipping hazardous materials or oversized goods?
Yes. Certain items (such as lithium batteries, aerosol sprays, and liquids) must travel via ground shipping and cannot be sent by air or express.

## How are fragile products protected during transit?
Fragile items are cushioned with recyclable bubble wrap, air pillows, and reinforced cartons tested against drop impacts.

## What should I do if a carrier marks my package as damaged during transit?
If tracking indicates that a package was damaged and returned to sender, contact customer support right away so a replacement can be dispatched.

## Can I schedule a specific delivery window or date?
ShopEase cannot set custom delivery windows, but carriers often offer scheduled delivery windows through their premium tracking platforms.

## Does ShopEase offer local locker pickup or click-and-collect options?
Yes. Eligible orders can select third-party parcel locker hubs at checkout instead of home delivery.

## What should I do if my tracking details have not updated in several days?
If tracking has not updated for 5 consecutive business days, reach out to customer support to open an official carrier trace investigation.

## Can shipping fees be refunded if an express order arrives late?
Yes. If an express or overnight shipment arrives past its guaranteed delivery window due to carrier delays (excluding extreme weather), shipping fees are refundable.
