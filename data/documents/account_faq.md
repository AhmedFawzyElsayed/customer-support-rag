# ShopEase Account FAQs

## How do I create an account?
Customers can create a ShopEase account using their email address, name, phone number, and password.

## How can I reset my password?
Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and enter your registered email address. A password-reset link will be sent to your email, and for security reasons that link is only valid for 30 minutes before it expires, after which you'll need to request a new one. Once you click the link, you'll be asked to create a new password that meets our minimum requirements (at least 8 characters, including one number and one symbol), and after saving it you'll be automatically logged out of all other active sessions as a security precaution, requiring you to sign back in on any other devices.

## Can I change my email address?
Yes. Customers can change their email address from Account Settings. For security purposes, the new email address must be verified before the change takes full effect, meaning a confirmation link is sent to the new address and your account will continue using the old email for login and notifications until that link is clicked. This verification step exists to prevent someone from locking you out of your own account by mistakenly entering an email address that isn't actually yours, and the change can be cancelled at any time before verification is completed simply by ignoring the confirmation email.

## How can I update my phone number?
Open Account Settings, select Personal Information, update your phone number, and save the changes.

## How do I delete my account?
Customers can request account deletion through Account Settings. Account deletion is permanent and cannot be reversed.

## Why is my account locked?
An account may be temporarily locked after multiple unsuccessful login attempts, which is a security measure designed to slow down anyone trying to guess your password. Wait 30 minutes and try again; the lock is automatically lifted after that period without requiring any action from you. If you're confident you're entering the correct password and the account remains locked well beyond 30 minutes, or if you suspect someone else may have triggered the lockout by trying to access your account, it's best to reset your password immediately afterward and contact customer support to review recent login activity.

## Can I create multiple accounts using the same email address?
No. Each ShopEase account must be associated with a unique email address.

## How do I enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)?
Go to Account Settings > Security, select "Two-Factor Authentication," and choose whether to receive one-time passcodes via SMS or an authenticator app.

## What should I do if I do not receive the password reset email?
Check your spam or junk folder. If it is still missing after 10 minutes, verify that you entered the correct email or submit another request.

## Can I sign in using third-party accounts like Google or Apple?
Yes. ShopEase supports social sign-in using verified Google, Apple, and Facebook credentials.

## How do I update my saved shipping and billing addresses?
Go to Account Settings > Address Book. From there, you can add new addresses, edit existing details, or remove outdated entries.

## Can I store multiple delivery addresses in my address book?
Yes. You can save multiple addresses and assign one as your default shipping destination.

## How do I manage email newsletters and marketing notifications?
Navigate to Account Settings > Communications Preferences to toggle marketing newsletters, deal alerts, and product recommendations on or off.

## How do I change the display language and preferred currency?
Scroll to the footer of any page or open Account Settings > Preferences to select your preferred language and display currency.

## What happens to my order history if my account is deleted?
Once an account is deleted, personal data and historical order records are permanently anonymized or removed in accordance with our data retention policy.

## Can I merge two separate ShopEase accounts into one?
No. ShopEase cannot merge multiple accounts, transfer purchase histories, or combine store credits between separate profiles.

## How do I add, update, or remove saved payment methods?
Visit Account Settings > Payment Methods to add new credit cards, update expiration dates, or delete saved payment profiles.

## How does ShopEase protect my personal data and payment details?
ShopEase uses industry-standard 256-bit SSL encryption and tokenized payment processing compliant with PCI-DSS standards.

## How can I review active login sessions or devices?
Go to Account Settings > Security > Active Sessions to view all devices currently logged into your account and sign out of remote sessions.

## What should I do if I detect unauthorized activity on my account?
Immediately reset your password, log out of all active sessions in Security settings, and notify ShopEase Customer Support.

## Can I shop on ShopEase as a guest without creating an account?
Yes. Guest checkout is available for most products, requiring only an email address, delivery details, and payment method.

## Can I convert a past guest order into an account?
Yes. If you create an account using the same email address used for guest checkout, your previous orders will automatically link to your order history.

## What should I do if I forget the email address linked to my account?
Contact ShopEase Customer Support with your full name, phone number, and a recent order confirmation number to locate your account.

## How do I change my profile name or display name?
Navigate to Account Settings > Personal Information, update your first or last name, and click "Save Changes."

## Does ShopEase charge any membership or account maintenance fees?
No. Standard ShopEase accounts are completely free to create and maintain.

## What are ShopEase Rewards points and where can I view my balance?
ShopEase Rewards points are earned on eligible purchases. You can view your current points balance and tier status under the "Rewards" tab in your account.

## Can I transfer ShopEase Rewards points to another user?
No. Rewards points and tier benefits are strictly non-transferable between accounts.

## How can I request an export of my personal data?
Submit a data export request under Account Settings > Privacy & Data. A downloadable archive will be sent to your verified email within 7 business days.

## Why was my account suspended or deactivated?
Accounts may be suspended for violations of our Terms of Service, suspected fraudulent activity, or payment dispute flags. Contact support for assistance.

## How do I reactivate a deactivated ShopEase account?
If you voluntarily deactivated your account, contact customer support within 90 days to verify your identity and restore access.

## What is the minimum age requirement to register an account?
Users must be at least 18 years old, or the age of legal majority in their jurisdiction, to create an account and place orders independently.
